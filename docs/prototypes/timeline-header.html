Faithful: the pin, the hand-off, the hairline appearing only while stuck, what each figure says, and the fact that nothing under your eyes moves when a month collapses.

Not faithful: pull-to-open — a browser has no rubber band, so tapping a bar is the gesture here. The blur is backdrop-filter, not Liquid Glass.

Watch for: at 11 things a month is one screenful, so the header barely pins. That threshold is real, not an artefact of the prototype.

Closing is capped rather than triggered by scrolling — two geometric triggers were tried and both are unreachable at 11 things. Set “Never close” and keep opening months to see what the cap prevents.
