Lanes, deep dive

Drag across the month to read it. Tap a lane's emoji or a chip to pick out one category. Tap a dot to open that charge. The controls change the design, not the data.

What this lies about
  • A browser has no press-and-hold timing, haptics or momentum like iOS. The "after a hold" option imitates the hold; on a phone, each day crossed would tick.
  • No Liquid Glass, no Dynamic Type. Accessibility text sizes aren't drawn, and lanes are where they'd bite.
  • A mouse drag is not a thumb: on a phone your finger covers the dot you're reading, which is why the readout sits above it.
  • Colours are a fixed set checked for colour blindness, assigned in the order categories were made. Past eight categories there are no more: the rest go grey. That's deliberate: the slider shows where colour runs out.
  • Invented bills. Months stacked runs July to December 2026 only.
  • The arrows' size and the 8pt gap between the two glass groups are drawn, not measured: Calendar has no pair like this.
  • Round 1 picks, 2026-09-24: lanes, lanes that shrink to fit, order as in Settings, empty categories folded into one line, a totals column, a readout above the finger that names only the charges, one month at a time.
