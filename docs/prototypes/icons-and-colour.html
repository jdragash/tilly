Tilly’s hand

Thirteen ways Tilly could draw a category’s picture, each on the four surfaces that lead with one: a timeline row, the lanes, the category picker and the new-category row. Every direction is drawn in light and dark, with what it takes to make a new category, how it scales, what it costs and which principles it leans on or strains. The brief is docs/briefs/icons-and-colour/brief.md.

All names and amounts are invented. Tap any icon to see a wobble. Panels marked stand-in show art Tilly doesn’t have: the licence or the source is real, the drawing on screen is mine, made to show the kind of thing.

Colour: paper and ink

The eight tokens as they are, then five ways of putting them on paper. The hues don’t change, only how they are laid down, so the validator’s pass on 2026-09-24 still describes the inks. It doesn’t yet describe the treated result: a wash or pencil shows paper through the colour, which lightens every ink and costs the pale ones (yellow, magenta) contrast against the ground first. Whichever treatment is chosen gets checked again as it renders. The Colour control at the top applies a treatment to every panel above.

What this prototype lies about

  • The emoji are your Mac’s, drawn by the browser. On iPhone they’re the same artwork, slightly different in rendering.
  • SF Symbols can’t be shown in a web page. H and I use glyphs I drew in their manner: one weight, rounded ends. The real set is more refined and has thousands more.
  • J shows no real Doodle Icons, K no real finger drawing, L no real Image Playground output, E no real Fluent 3D. Each is a stand-in for the kind of thing.
  • The rough lines are SVG filters and jittered paths in a browser. In the app they’d be a Metal shader or a SwiftUI Canvas, and they would look close to this but not the same.
  • There’s no Dynamic Type here: the accessibility column is drawn at roughly the largest size, not measured.
  • The boil runs at 8 frames a second on a screen of 13 at once. One boiling row in a real list reads very differently from a wall of them.
