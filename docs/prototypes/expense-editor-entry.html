Tilly — adding and editing

Prototype · 22 September 2026 · adding and editing, and the Calendar-style shell

appearance
opening a row

"Edit" opens the editor on the keypad; ✓ asks which charges you mean. "View first" opens a page about the charge, as Calendar does: "Change this charge" or Edit, and ✓ never asks, because the button you chose already said.

the store

"In use" has invented bills going back to a car insurance renewal last October. "First run" has no expenses and no categories, so your first expense makes your first category. Switching starts the store over.

try

Tap a row to edit that charge. Change the amount and tap ✓: it asks whether you mean this charge or the ones after it. Type 0 for a free month. The red trash button deletes what's ahead, or everything. Scroll down into history: each month's header takes over the top row, beside +. Tap September to come back. "Today" is fixed at 17 September 2026. On a computer you can type the amount on your own keyboard.

What this lies about. A browser is not iOS.
  • no system keyboards: the grey blocks stand in, and the emoji grid stands in for the emoji keyboard; it isn't a proposed set
  • no wheel physics or haptics: the wheel is scroll snapping
  • no Liquid Glass, sheet detents, or a sheet that follows your thumb
  • no Dynamic Type: at accessibility sizes three equal buttons can't hold, and what they become is unsolved
  • no VoiceOver, rubber-band overscroll or iOS momentum
  • a year ahead rather than five, and your place isn't kept between visits
  • no system menus or dialogs: the scope menu and the delete question are drawn
