Also new in round 2
  • All bills has Monthly / Yearly at the top. Monthly divides a bill’s year by twelve, so a yearly bill reads as its share of a month.
  • Where a row’s figure isn’t what is actually charged, the line beside the name says what is: “€640 yearly” in Monthly, “€1,150 monthly” in Yearly.
  • No bill count under a category, and no “a year” under each total: the control says which.
  • The chips follow the cards, highest first; cards and the bills in them already did.
  • “Extras” means anything not charged every month: renewals, quarterly bills, things you plan to buy.
  • With everything shown, a month’s label is its total, bold when €300 or more comes on top.
What this prototype lies about
  • No Liquid Glass: the glass is a blur and a tint.
  • No Dynamic Type; sizes are fixed at the default.
  • Browser scrolling, not iOS momentum or rubber-banding.
  • No pinch to zoom out; only the buttons and taps.
  • Tapping a bill doesn't open the editor; + and settings do nothing.
  • The categories lens (lanes) isn't here.
